About us

A new company with veteran experience

Advantys AI is a consultancy and venture builder based in Andorra. We apply artificial intelligence to the operations of transport, logistics and agri-food companies, to AI governance in schools and to sales automation.

Jacob Ruiz

Founder and CEO

He has spent more than twenty-five years selling, implementing and explaining technology to companies that have no time for jargon. He started out leading sales teams in express transport and renewable energy, so he knows operations from the inside: the lorry that is waiting, the delivery note that never arrives and the customer on the phone.

He then specialised in consultative selling of CRM and automation, ran one of the leading Spanish-speaking Zoho partners and, from Hong Kong, built an international partner network for one of Meta’s leading business messaging providers. He studied at ESIC Business & Marketing School.

At Advantys he runs the diagnostics and leads every project.
