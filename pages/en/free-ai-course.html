The idea

AI does not take your job. It takes the tedious tasks.

You will learn to hand repetitive work over to AI: emails, summaries, reports and minutes. The judgement and the final say stay with you.

Certificate and the EU AI Act

A certificate for you. A record for your company.

Article 4 of the EU AI Act asks companies that use AI to take measures to promote training for their staff. Since the Digital Omnibus, the requirement is proportionate: nobody is expected to be an expert, but you must have done something reasonable and be able to show it.

  • A real training measure. Four hours of practical content, with a quiz for each block.
  • A record of who has been trained. Each certificate shows the name, hours, syllabus and date.
  • The basics of the law, in block 4. Which data must not go into an AI and how to check what it returns.

The course does not replace any other obligations the Act may place on each company. For details of what Article 4 requires and how to document it, see this guide to AI literacy (in Spanish).

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The course is worth €250 and is funded by Advantys AI. You do not need to give a card or pay anything at the end.

What do I need to take it?

You need to be able to use email and a web browser. We work with the free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, so you do not have to pay for any tool.

How long is it?

About four hours in total: 21 videos of around 10 minutes each, split into four blocks. You go at your own pace, on a computer or phone.

Does it help with the EU AI Act?

Article 4 asks companies that use AI to take measures to promote training for their staff, in proportion to each person's role. This course is one of those measures, and the certificate records who has been trained and when. It does not replace any other obligations the Act may place on each company.

Can I sign up my whole team?

Yes. Each person signs up with their own email and gets their own certificate at the end. That gives you a training record for your whole workforce.

What languages is it in?

Spanish and Catalan. The whole course is recorded in both languages and you choose yours when you sign up. It is not available in English.
