The problem

Where margin goes in a transport company

Not in the freight rate. It goes in everything that happens between the order coming in and the invoice going out, and it almost never shows up in any report.

Goods nobody can locate

A misplaced pallet means phone calls, searches round the warehouse and sometimes an order that has to be picked again. The customer asks and nobody knows straight away.

Routes and groupage planned by hand

It depends on one person and their spreadsheet. When they are off, lorries go out half-loaded and run kilometres nobody gets paid for.

Paper delivery notes

The driver hands it in, someone types it up, someone checks it and someone invoices. Every copy is another chance for an error that someone has to chase later.

Docks and waiting times nobody measures

Lorries waiting at the dock, cold rooms full or empty at the wrong time. If it is not measured, nobody knows what it costs.

What we do

What AI can do in your operation

We do not sell a closed platform. These are the levers that win back the most margin in the sector. Which ones make sense for you is decided with data, not with a demo.

Pallet location with computer vision

Cameras that recognise every pallet as it comes in, moves and goes out, and record its location without anyone writing it down.

Goods traceability

The status of every shipment in one place, from collection to delivery, with automatic alerts to the customer when something is delayed.

Routes and groupage

Route and load proposals based on the day's orders. The traffic manager decides; AI takes the repetitive part off their plate.

Delivery notes and paperwork

Automatic reading of delivery notes and CMRs, digital signature on delivery and straight through to invoicing, with no retyping.

Frequently asked questions

Before you take the step

Do I have to change my transport or warehouse management software?

No. We start from what you already use. If your software can be connected to, we build on top of it. If not, we tell you clearly what you would gain by changing it and what it would cost, so you can decide.

Do we need to install cameras or sensors?

Only if it pays off. Computer vision to locate pallets or monitor docks is one option, not a starting point. First we measure what the problem costs you today, and if the investment does not pay for itself, we do not propose it.

Where do we start?

With the Operations Diagnostic: four weeks, a fixed price and a report on what to do first, what it costs and what it saves. With that report you decide whether we keep working together or you do it yourself.

Do you work with small companies?

We work with operators that have their own fleet, warehouse or traffic office and a problem that costs them money every week. If you are three people and one lorry, it is probably not worth it for you yet. If in doubt, tell us and we will give you a straight answer.

Where do you work?

In Spain and Andorra. The diagnostic combines sessions at your site with remote work.
