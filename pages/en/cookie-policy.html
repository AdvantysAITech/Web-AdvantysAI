1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, tablet or mobile phone) when you visit them. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your language or session preferences, which makes your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

This Cookie Policy applies to the website www.advantys.ai, operated by Advantys Ai, SL, and has been drawn up in accordance with the applicable Andorran data protection and electronic communications legislation.

2. Types of cookies we use

By ownership:

  • First-party cookies: generated and managed directly by Advantys Ai.
  • Third-party cookies: placed by third-party service providers (Google, Meta, etc.).

By purpose:

  • Technical or strictly necessary cookies: essential for the website to work. They do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies: allow us to measure the number of visits and analyse user behaviour in order to improve the site. They require consent.
  • Marketing cookies: used to show you relevant advertising based on your profile. They require consent.

By duration:

  • Session cookies: deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: remain for the period defined in each cookie.

5. How can you manage or disable cookies?

You can withdraw your consent or change your cookie preferences at any time through the website’s cookie settings panel, or directly from your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, search and services → Cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the proper functioning of the website.

To reject Google Analytics cookies: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

To manage Meta cookies: facebook.com/privacy/policies/cookies

6. International transfers

Some of our cookie providers (Google, Meta) are located in the United States. These international transfers are carried out with the safeguards required by Andorran data protection legislation, including, where applicable, standard contractual clauses or other recognised safeguard mechanisms.
