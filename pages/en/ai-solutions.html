Where to start

Every sector loses money in different places

No single AI works for everything. That is why we work in a few sectors and know their problems up close.

  • Transport and logistics. Margin leaks through goods that cannot be found, routes and groupage planned by hand, and delivery notes typed twice. Here AI helps locate pallets with computer vision, propose routes and loads from the day’s orders, and read documents without retyping them.
  • Agri-food. Fruit and vegetable cooperatives and packhouses that settle with growers on a warehouse average and cannot prove the origin of each batch. Tree-to-pallet traceability connects the sensors, the grading machine and settlement on real quality.
  • Education. Private and state-funded private schools that need to decide, document and prove how AI is used in each activity, with use levels, a use agreement between school, teacher and student, and traceable evidence.

In transport and agri-food, everything starts with the Operations Diagnostic: four weeks, a fixed price and a report on what to do first, what it costs and how much it saves. If you want a rough idea first, the free self-assessment takes ten minutes.
