5. Rights of data subjects

You may exercise the following rights over your personal data at any time:

  • Restriction: request that we restrict the processing of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise these rights, send an email to contacto@advantys.ai stating the right you wish to exercise and attaching a copy of your identity document. We will respond to your request within the maximum period established by law.
