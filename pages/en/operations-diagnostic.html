The problem

In transport and agri-food, margin is not lost in the sale. It is lost in operations.

You see it every day and it no longer surprises you. That is exactly why it keeps costing money.

Pallets that go missing

Someone looks for them, someone calls the customer and someone redoes the order. Nobody records how much time that took.

Routes planned by hand

Every morning the same spreadsheet, the same person, and the same problem on the day that person is off.

Delivery notes typed in twice

From paper to the system, and from the system to the invoice. Every time someone copies data, an error slips in that someone has to chase later.

Cold rooms and docks nobody measures

If you do not know how long a lorry waits at the dock or how long a cold room stays full, you cannot know what it costs you.

Do the maths. One hour a day planning routes or re-entering delivery notes adds up to more than 200 hours a year. Per person. And it shows up in no report, because nobody is looking.

What usually happens

And when someone finally decides to act, they start at the wrong end

Software is bought before the process is understood

The tool arrives, the team keeps working as before and the problem stays where it was. Now with one more licence to pay for.

Hourly consulting that never ends

Meetings, endless documents and an invoice that grows every month. In the end nobody knows what to do on Monday.

Shop-window AI

A flashy demo, a proof of concept that never reaches production, and the feeling of having paid for a video.

The problem is not the technology. It is starting with the technology.

Payment is 100% on signing, by bank transfer, and the kick-off session takes place within the following 10 working days. From there, four weeks. If schedules or information are delayed, the timeline moves by the same amount.
