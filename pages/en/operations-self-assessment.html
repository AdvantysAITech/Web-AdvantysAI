Before you start

What does this self-assessment measure?

It measures the most common signs that an operation is losing money: work standing still, data typed in twice, decisions without data, reliance on specific people, software that does not talk to each other, and late invoicing.

Does it work for any company?

It is designed for companies with physical operations: transport, logistics, warehousing and agri-food. The questions make sense in any company with orders, deliveries and invoices.

How is it different from the Operations Diagnostic?

The self-assessment tells you whether there are losses and in which area. We carry out the Operations Diagnostic ourselves over four weeks: we measure what each problem costs, what to fix first and with what investment.
